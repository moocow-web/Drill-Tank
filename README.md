# Drill tank
## About
This project is a RC screw/drill tank based off of the soviet era ZIL2906. It uses 2 opposite augers for locomotion. If they rotate in opposite directions, they will propel the
vehicle forward, whereas if the rotate in the same direction, the vehicle will strafe left/right. 
## Screw tanks 
Screw tanks were originally invented because they can traverse almost any terrain, are amphibious, and have immense towing force. They were first made in the USSR 
to rescue cosmonauts from their capsule in the deep siberian wilderness. Today, they have faded into obscurity :(
## How to make one yourself
To build one yourself, follow this guide
1. Purchase all parts from the BOM section
2. Download the .stl Files from this Repo, and print them in pla
3. Cut your aluminum extrusions into the following:
  1. 12in x 2
  2. 20in x 2
  3. 5in x 4
4. Assemble your aluminum extrusions following [this](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/9eef6bfda31257e1ca530c9f/w/33c118a19c1b1a7c49c2ec95/e/7efc65a16f6c0f600e8bdadf "Onshape") assembly
5. Mount the motors, bearings and other parts following the aforementioned assembly

## BOM
- 7 (8 for extra wiggle room)ft of REV 1in extrusion
- 2 x 37GB520 50RPM motors
- 2 x 12v ESCs
- 2 x 608 bearings
- Any 3s lipo
- (whatever connector your lipo is) y-splitter
- Any RC transmitter + receiver
